Privacy Policy for XTN Chat

Last updated: November 24, 2026

XTN Chat ("the App", package hu.xtn.chat) is developed by DipaStudio. XTN Chat is a private, invite-only messenger that connects to a self-hosted server at https://chat.xtn.hu. This privacy policy describes what information the App handles, why it is needed, and how you can export or delete your data.

1. Information We Collect and How It Is Used

The App only collects information required to operate messaging, media sharing, push notifications, and voice or video calls:

  • Account and profile information: When you sign in with Google, the server verifies your Google ID token and stores your Google account identifier, email address, display name, chosen username, optional profile photo, optional status text, account creation date, and last-seen timestamp. Your email address is kept private and is not shown to other users.
  • Messages and media: Direct and group conversations, text messages, replies, reactions, forwarded and starred markers, delivery and read receipts, and attachments you choose to send (photos, videos, voice messages, and files, along with generated thumbnails and placeholders). This data is stored on chat.xtn.hu to deliver messages and sync your chats across your devices.
  • Link previews: When a text message contains a web link, the server fetches the page title, description, and preview image server-side and caches them on chat.xtn.hu so recipients' devices do not contact third-party websites directly.
  • Device and session records: Active signed-in sessions (device platform, hashed refresh token, and last-active timestamp) and your Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) token so the server can deliver push notifications and incoming call alerts.
  • Voice and video calls: Calls use WebRTC with encrypted media streams (DTLS-SRTP), connected directly between participants when possible or relayed through the self-hosted TURN server on chat.xtn.hu. Call audio and video are never recorded or stored. When a call ends, the App may upload an anonymous technical event timeline (timestamps, signalling states, end reason, and app version, with no user ID attached) to diagnose call reliability issues.

XTN Chat contains no advertisements, no advertising identifiers, no third-party analytics SDKs, and no crash-reporting SDKs. The App does not read your phone's contacts or collect your location.

2. Local Data Storage and Security

  • XTN Chat is offline-first. Conversations, messages, member profiles, queued outgoing messages, cached media, and app preferences (such as theme, notification sounds, and download preferences) are stored locally on your device in an on-device Room/SQLite database and standard Android preferences.
  • Local databases and preferences are excluded from Android cloud backups.
  • All communication between the App and chat.xtn.hu is encrypted in transit using HTTPS and WSS (TLS).

3. Third-Party Services

We do not sell or share your personal data with advertisers or data brokers. The App uses the following Google services strictly for core functionality:

  • Google Sign-In (Android Credential Manager): Used to authenticate your account during sign-in.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM): Used to wake the App for new messages and incoming calls when it is in the background. Message push payloads include the sender name, message type, and a short preview or attachment metadata so notifications can appear immediately.
  • Google Play Services Code Scanner: Used only when you choose to scan a QR code for a profile or invite.

Google's handling of data processed by these services is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Permissions

  • Internet and network state: Required to connect to chat.xtn.hu, sync messages, and maintain calls across Wi-Fi and cellular networks.
  • Notifications and full-screen intent: Used to display message notifications and full-screen incoming call screens.
  • Microphone, audio settings, Bluetooth, and Telecom calls: Used to record voice messages, place voice and video calls, route call audio to headsets or Bluetooth devices, and integrate with Android's system call UI.
  • Camera (optional): Used only when you take a photo or video inside the App or use video during a call.
  • Photos and videos (optional): Used only when you choose media from your device to send in a chat or set as an avatar.
  • Foreground service, wake lock, and battery optimization exemption (optional): Used to keep active calls running reliably with the screen off and to help prevent delayed notifications or missed calls.

5. Data Export, Retention, and Account Deletion

Your account, messages, and uploaded media remain stored on chat.xtn.hu for as long as your account is active so your chat history stays synced.

  • In-app controls: You can delete individual messages for everyone, hide messages on your device, clear cached media in Settings → Storage, sign out or revoke any signed-in device in Settings → Active devices, and export a JSON copy of your conversations and messages at any time in Settings → Export data.
  • Local data removal: Signing out clears account data on the device. You can also erase all locally stored data at any time by clearing the App's data in Android Settings or by uninstalling the App.
  • Permanent account deletion: To delete your XTN Chat account and associated server data (your profile, email address, active device sessions, FCM tokens, invites, and uploaded media/messages), send an email to dupen210@gmail.com from the Google email address associated with your account (or include your @username) with the subject XTN Chat Account Deletion. Requests are completed within 30 days. Anonymous call diagnostic logs contain no user identifier and cannot be linked to your account.

6. Children's Privacy

XTN Chat is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the applicable minimum age in your jurisdiction), and DipaStudio does not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has created an account, please contact us and we will delete the account and its data.
