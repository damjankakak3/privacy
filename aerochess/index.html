Privacy Policy for AeroChess

Last updated: November 6, 2026

AeroChess ("the App") is developed by DipaStudio. AeroChess lets two people play chess on two Android devices that are near each other, without an internet connection. This privacy policy describes how the App handles user information.

1. Information Collection and Usage

  • DipaStudio does not collect, receive, or store any personal information from the App. There is no game server.
  • The App does not request internet access (android.permission.INTERNET is not declared).
  • No user accounts, registration, or logins are required.
  • No analytics tools, advertising networks, or third-party tracking software are integrated into the App.

2. Local Data Storage

The App stores the following on your device only: the player name you enter, your match history (opponent name, result, how the game ended, date, and material score), and the state of an interrupted game so it can be resumed. This data never leaves your device, except as described in section 3.

3. Playing With a Nearby Device

  • While you host or search for a game, your player name can be seen by nearby devices running AeroChess.
  • During a game, the data needed to play (moves, board position, and remaining clock time) is sent directly to your opponent's device.
  • Device discovery and the connection are handled by Google Nearby Connections, part of Google Play services, using Bluetooth and Wi-Fi. Google's handling of this is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Permissions

  • Nearby devices (Bluetooth scan, advertise, and connect; nearby Wi-Fi devices): used to find your opponent's device and connect to it.
  • Location: required by Google Nearby Connections to scan for nearby devices. The App does not read, store, or share your location.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Because no user data is sent to DipaStudio, no personal data is retained by DipaStudio. You can delete your match history in the App's Settings, and you can erase all locally stored data at any time by clearing the App's data in Android Settings or by uninstalling the App.

6. Children's Privacy

The App does not collect personal data from any user, including children under the age of 13 (or applicable age in your jurisdiction).
