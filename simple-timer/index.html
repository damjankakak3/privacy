Privacy Policy for Simple Timer

Last updated: September 22, 2026

Simple Timer ("the App") is developed by DipaStudio. This privacy policy describes how the App handles user information.

1. Information Collection and Usage

Simple Timer is a 100% offline application.

  • The App does not collect, track, store, transmit, or share any personal information, device identifiers, or usage data.
  • The App does not request or use internet access (android.permission.INTERNET is not declared or used).
  • No user accounts, registration, or logins are required.
  • No analytics tools, advertising networks, or third-party tracking software are integrated into the App.

2. Local Data Storage

All user configurations (such as timer duration, display format, digit color, sound toggle, and flash toggle) are saved locally on your device using standard Android preferences (SharedPreferences). This data never leaves your device.

3. Data Retention and Deletion

Because the App does not transmit or store any user data on remote servers, no personal data is retained by DipaStudio. You can erase all locally stored preferences at any time by clearing the App's data in Android Settings or by uninstalling the App from your device.

4. Permissions

Simple Timer does not request any sensitive permissions (such as camera, location, contacts, or storage). The App only requests the screen to stay on (FLAG_KEEP_SCREEN_ON) while the timer is actively open in the foreground.

5. Children's Privacy

The App does not collect personal data from any user, including children under the age of 13 (or applicable age in your jurisdiction).
